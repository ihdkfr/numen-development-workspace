   - Try a faction war and the war seasons (FactionWarService, build 136).
   - After each one, open View > Output and look for red lines. Press Stop.

   - Try a raid, a seat fight and a war. They were switched off at the last check, so turn them on first.
   - Try CreatorService and buy something in the Soul Candy shop (build 133).
   - Check View > Output for red lines after each one.

   - If you see red text in Output, copy it into Notes and say which item caused it.

   - Press Save note.
6. Approve this task when everything works.
   - Tick each checklist item that you played and that worked.
   - Attach a capture of anything that looked wrong with + Attach result.
   - Press Submit for review. Leave failed items unticked and name them in Notes.
