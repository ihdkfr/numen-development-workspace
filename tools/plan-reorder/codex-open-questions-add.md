
## New decisions for Phase 02 (added 13 Oct)
Answer each one in its task in Phase 02.

- **Core loop and pacing (T02.01)** What a player does in a normal session, where XP comes from, and roughly how many hours to Shikai, Bankai, Adjuchas and Vasto Lorde.
- **What's in version 1 (T02.02)** What the first public version needs and what waits, for example 30 Zanpakuto instead of 100.
- **Knock-downs and death (T02.05)** Can others grip, carry or finish a downed player? What do you lose when you die? What happens if someone leaves mid-fight?
- **Races, clans and Vizard (T02.06)** Which races at launch, clans yes or no, Vizard path yes or no.
- **Money plan (T02.07)** What you'll sell, what you'll never sell, and the prices.
- **Server size (T02.08)** Players per server, and whether to sell private servers.
- **Names and copyright (T02.09)** Which Bleach names, logos, music and looks to keep or change.
- **Maturity answers (T02.10)** How much blood and violence the game shows, for Roblox's questionnaire.
