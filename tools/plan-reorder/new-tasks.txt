NEW:death-rules | Decide what happens when you're knocked down or die
W: Can others grip, carry or finish a downed player? What do you lose when you die? What happens if someone leaves mid-fight? Every top Bleach game has rules for this.
1. Decide if others can grip, carry or finish you.
   - Grip means holding a knocked-down player to finish them. Carry means picking them up and moving them.
   - Finishing adds tension, but it can make new players feel bullied.
   - Example answer: "Grip and finish only in PvP zones. Carry anywhere, so friends can rescue you."
   - Write your answer in this task's Notes.
2. Decide what you lose when you die.
   - Options: nothing, some money, some XP, a dropped item, or a short wait before you come back.
   - Big losses make fights scary but can make players quit. Most games use a small loss.
   - Example answer: "Lose 10% of the money you carry. Keep all items and XP."
3. Decide what happens if someone leaves mid-fight.
   - Some players leave the game to dodge a death. This is called combat logging.
   - Example answer: "If you leave within 15 seconds of being hit, you count as dead when you come back."
   - Write how many seconds after a hit still counts as "in a fight".
4. Write the rules as a short list.
   - Turn your three answers into 4 to 8 numbered rules in Notes.
   - Read them as a brand new player would. Fix anything unclear.
   - Press Save note.
D: The rules are a short written list.
