S11.01 | Check what's built: menus, sound and looks
W: Some menus, sounds and visual polish are already in the game. They only count as done when you have played them and they work.
1. Open Studio with the current build (see S01.01).
2. Play each item in the checklist.
3. Read the Output window after each one.
4. Write anything broken in a note here, or as a new task.
5. Approve this task when everything works.
D: Every item has been played, and it is approved or its problems are written in a note.

S11.02 | Finish the HUD and menus
W: Make the HUD (the on-screen bars and buttons) and menus clean, simple and anime-styled.
1. Make separate HUD modes for exploring and fighting.
2. Make the most important info easiest to see.
3. Join menus that overlap.
4. Guide players without lots of markers.
5. Show status, bounty and rank clearly.
D: The HUD warns you of danger without covering the game.

S11.03 | Finish the character creator
W: Let players make their character look how they want, without looks changing their power.
1. Polish the human creator.
2. Show Hollow forms and masks.
3. Link Bankai looks to the creator.
4. Add the wardrobe and recolours.
D: Looks never change hitboxes or fighting power.

S11.04 | Add all the sound and music
W: Missing or placeholder sound is the biggest quality gap. Add real sounds and our own music everywhere.
1. Add footsteps and hit sounds.
2. Add movement and spiritual pressure sounds.
3. Add Hollow sounds.
4. Add transformation sounds.
5. Add background sound for each realm.
6. Add our own music, never ripped anime audio.
D: No big moment in the game is silent or uses a placeholder.

S11.05 | Add camera and accessibility settings
W: Settings for cutscene camera, screen shake, flashing and colour-blind players, without changing fight timing.
1. Add Full, Reduced and Off cutscene camera.
2. Add a screen shake slider.
3. Add flash reduction.
4. Make colours clear for colour-blind players.
5. Fix the camera going through walls.
D: Settings change only how it looks, never how fights play.

S11.06 | Build stances and weapon looks
W: Stances and sword looks let players show off their style before releasing a power, without any hidden advantage.
1. Make stance styles that change idle poses only.
2. Add sealed sword looks that fit the sword rules.
3. Add carry styles for each race.
4. Check nothing clips on custom bodies or phones.
D: Players look unique before releasing, and looks never change reach or speed.
